King of Curses

Ryomen Sukuna

The Undisputed Apex

1,000 years ago, Ryomen Sukuna was a human sorcerer so powerful that even after death, he could not be destroyed. His fingers became cursed objects of immeasurable power. Reincarnated inside Yuji Itadori, he watches, waits, and acts only for his own amusement — answering to no one.

✦ Age: 1000+ 🗡️ Technique: Cleave & Dismantle 👁️ 4 Arms, 4 Eyes 💀 Grade: Special Grade ∞
His Nature "I don't have allies. I don't have enemies. There is only what amuses me — and what does not."

Powers & Abilities

Sukuna's Arsenal

🗡️
Dismantle
An offensive slashing attack of pure cursed energy, Dismantle cuts through anything — objects, curses, and sorcerers alike.
⚡
Cleave
Adjusts automatically to the target's durability and cursed energy to deliver a precisely lethal slash — no more, no less.
🏯
Malevolent Shrine
Sukuna's domain expansion — an open-air shrine that continuously activates Dismantle and Cleave on everything within range, with no escape.
🔥
Fire Arrow
A technique stolen from Jogo — Sukuna wields fire with far greater power than the original user, demonstrating his absurd talent.
