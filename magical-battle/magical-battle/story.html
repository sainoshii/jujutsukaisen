A tale of hope and despair — where every wish has a cost, and every magical girl hides a tragedy.

Story Arc

The Timeline of Fate

Episode 1–2
A New World
Madoka and Gojo encounter a wounded creature named Kyubey and the mysterious transfer student Homura. They are drawn into the world of magical girls.
Episode 3
The First Loss
Mami Tomoe — the elegant veteran — is brutally killed by a witch, shattering all illusions about the safety of this magical life. Nothing is safe.
Episode 7–8
Gojo's Resolve
Gojo discovers the truth behind the sorcerer world — sacrifices are demanded from the young. He resolves to change everything, no matter the cost.
Episode 9
Kyoko's Sacrifice
Kyoko refuses to abandon Gojo even in the darkest hour. She uses her barrier ability to protect him, sacrificing herself so he can fight on.
Episode 10
Homura's Loops
Homura reveals the truth: she has relived this same month countless times, losing Madoka every single time. She has become cold, efficient, and broken by loss.
Episode 12
Madoka's Wish
Madoka makes the ultimate wish — to erase all witches before they are born, across all timelines. She becomes a transcendent concept: the Law of Cycles.

"I will destroy all despair and replace it with hope."

— Madoka Kaname
