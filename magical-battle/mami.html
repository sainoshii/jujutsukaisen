Jujutsu Sorcerer

Nobara Kugisaki

The Iron-Willed Hammer

A fiercely confident first-year sorcerer from rural Morioka who came to Tokyo to live life on her own terms. Armed with a hammer and nails, Nobara's Straw Doll technique is deceptively deadly — and her unbreakable spirit makes her a force no curse can shake.

✦ Age: 16 🔨 Weapon: Hammer & Nails 🪆 Technique: Straw Doll 💛 Grade: Grade 3 → 1
Her Creed "I'm not going to bend my life around other people. I'm going to live exactly how I want."

Powers & Abilities

Nobara's Arsenal

🪆
Straw Doll Technique
Nobara uses a straw doll as a cursed vessel — driving nails through it transmits cursed energy damage directly to the target's body.
🔨
Hairpin
She fires nails like bullets, imbued with cursed energy, using her hammer in close combat or her technique at range simultaneously.
💀
Resonance
By connecting to the soul through a fragment of the enemy's body, she channels Resonance — a devastating technique that bypasses physical defenses.
🔥
Unbreakable Will
Nobara's mental fortitude is her true weapon — she never wavers, even against cursed spirits or impossible odds.

"My aesthetic and my life — I'll never compromise on either."
