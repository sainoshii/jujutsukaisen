Jujutsu Sorcerer

Satoru Gojo

The Strongest

The most powerful jujutsu sorcerer in existence, Gojo's mastery of the Limitless technique and the Six Eyes make him nearly untouchable. A teacher at Tokyo Jujutsu High, he carries the weight of reshaping the entire sorcerer world on his shoulders — with a smile.

✦ Age: 28 ♾️ Technique: Limitless 👁️ Six Eyes 💥 Power: Infinite
His Goal "I'm gonna change the jujutsu world. To do that, I need to raise the right kind of people."

Powers & Abilities

Gojo's Arsenal

♾️
Infinity / Limitless
Gojo manipulates space at the atomic level, creating an infinite barrier that slows anything approaching him to a halt.
🌀
Blue & Red
Cursed technique lapse (Blue) attracts matter; reversal (Red) repels it with explosive force. Both are devastating in combat.
💜
Hollow Purple
The convergence of Blue and Red — an erasure technique that eliminates everything in its path from existence.
👁️
Six Eyes
Rare ocular ability granting near-perfect perception of cursed energy, allowing Gojo to use Limitless with virtually zero consumption.
