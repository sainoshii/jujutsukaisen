Jujutsu Sorcerer

Hajime Kashimo

The Lightning God of 400 Years

A legendary sorcerer from 400 years in the past who made a binding vow with Kenjaku to be reincarnated and face Ryomen Sukuna — the one person he believed could give him the battle he craved his entire life. Cold, calculating, and crackling with lethal electricity.

✦ Age: 400+ yrs ⚡ Technique: Genju Kohaku 🌩️ Element: Lightning 💀 Grade: Special Grade
His Purpose "I've lived 400 years for one moment — to fight the strongest being who ever existed."
