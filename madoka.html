Jujutsu Sorcerer

Yuta Okkotsu

Special Grade — Immeasurable

Once a timid boy haunted by the powerful cursed spirit of his childhood friend Rika, Yuta grew into one of the strongest sorcerers alive. Trained overseas by Miguel under Gojo's guidance, he returned as a fearsome special grade — able to copy any cursed technique and wielding Rika's boundless power.

✦ Age: 17 ⚔️ Weapon: Katana + Rika 🌸 Grade: Special Grade ♾️ Power: Copy
His Drive "I want to survive — and protect the people important to me, no matter what it takes."

Powers & Abilities

Yuta's Arsenal

📋
Copycat Technique
Yuta can copy and reproduce any cursed technique he has observed, making him a nearly unlimited arsenal in battle.
👻
Rika — Queen of Curses
The boundless cursed spirit of Rika Orimoto manifests as an overwhelming force, capable of annihilating almost anything.
⚔️
Katana Mastery
Yuta channels immense cursed energy through his blade, delivering strikes with speed and power that few can match.
💊
Reverse Cursed Technique
Yuta can heal himself and others using reversed cursed energy — even bringing someone back from the brink of death.
